# Priority client engagements

Suggested starting points for application hosting organizations modernizing a legacy VMware datacenter. The order depends on your operational risks and workload dependencies. See [engagement models](engagements.html) for how projects are scoped.

| Priority | Offering | Business value |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Datacenter health and risk assessment](services/datacenter-modernization.html) | Identify critical risks affecting hosted services |
| 2 | [VMware-to-Hyper-V/System Center migration](services/datacenter-modernization.html) | Migrate VMware workloads to Hyper-V and System Center |
| 3 | [Application hosting architecture optimization](services/application-hosting.html) | Improve reliability, performance, scalability, and isolation |
| 4 | [Active Directory security and escalation-path analysis](services/identity-privileged-access-security.html) | Reduce privileged compromise and lateral movement risk |
| 5 | [Privileged administration architecture](services/identity-privileged-access-security.html) | Implement PAWs, tiering, JEA, and controlled delegation |
| 6 | [Microsoft infrastructure modernization](services/microsoft-server-infrastructure.html) | Reduce unsupported dependencies and technical debt |
| 7 | [Infrastructure and deployment automation](services/powershell-automation.html) | Improve deployment consistency and operational efficiency |
| 8 | [Backup and disaster recovery readiness](services/backup-disaster-recovery.html) | Strengthen recoverability and continuity |
| 9 | [Azure and hybrid-cloud strategy](services/cloud-hybrid-infrastructure.html) | Evaluate target platforms and economic tradeoffs |
| 10 | [SPLA and hosting licensing strategy](services/licensing-hosting-strategy.html) | Improve licensing architecture and cost visibility |
| 11 | [Monitoring and operational reliability](services/monitoring-performance-reliability.html) | Improve issue detection and response |
| 12 | [Ongoing infrastructure advisory](services/strategic-advisory-architecture.html) | Guide architecture decisions, review vendor proposals, and plan migrations |
